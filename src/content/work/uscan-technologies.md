## The job

USCAN is my dad's IT company. I worked there part-time all through high school. It's where I first learned networking and support, hands-on.

## What I did

- **Remote support.** Troubleshot client software issues over TeamViewer and Windows Remote Desktop, with every issue tracked and updated in Jira.
- **Cabling contracts.** Worked jobs in office and industrial buildings: terminating CAT6 at the wall plate, installing patch panels and server racks, and setting up VoIP phones.

## What mattered

Most of the job was explaining problems to people who just wanted their computer to work. Showing up on site, finding the fault, and leaving it running is still how I think about infrastructure work.
