## The job

I was a pharmacy technician through the pandemic, which meant prescriptions on a normal day and a vaccine clinic once vaccines arrived.

## What I did

- **Vaccine clinic.** Vaccine preparation, client check-in and check-out. Reworking that flow cut wait times by **43%**.
- **Inventory and bookings.** Managed COVID-19 vaccine inventory and appointment sign-ups in Salesforce and spreadsheets.
- **Dispensary.** Processed prescriptions and worked with healthcare providers on patient issues and refills.
