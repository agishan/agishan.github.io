## The job

I was a pharmacy technician through the pandemic, which meant prescriptions on a normal day and a vaccine clinic once vaccines arrived.

## What I did

- **Ran the vaccine clinic's logistics.** Vaccine preparation, client check-in and check-out. Reworking that flow cut wait times by **43%**.
- **Kept track of supply and bookings.** Managed COVID-19 vaccine inventory and appointment sign-ups in Salesforce and spreadsheets.
- **The day job.** Processed prescriptions and worked with healthcare providers on patient issues and refills.

## What mattered

Looking back, this was my first scheduling problem. A line of people, a fixed number of doses and staff, and a process that could be faster. The same shape shows up later at [Cambridge Memorial](/work/cambridge-memorial-hospital) and in my [clinic scheduling](/projects/clinic-room-scheduling) work.
