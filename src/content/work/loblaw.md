## The job

I was on Loblaw's Supply Chain Process Improvement team, working on shrink: inventory lost to theft, damage and spoilage.

## What I did

- **Shrink analysis.** I analyzed **4.3M+ SKUs** across every Loblaw store in Ontario with SQL and Python to find which banners and categories carried the most shrink risk, so reduction effort could go where it would pay.
- **Access migration.** I rebuilt **11+** legacy MS Access applications as Python/SQL scripts. Reports ran **93%** faster.
- **Report automation.** I built scheduled Python reports (pandas, OpenPyXL, Oracle, GCP) that an offshore team relied on, saving them **115+ hours** a year.
