## The job

I was on Loblaw's Supply Chain Process Improvement team, working on shrink: inventory lost to theft, damage and spoilage. It's one of the biggest costs a grocer can actually control.

## What I did

- **Found where the loss was.** I analyzed **4.3M+ SKUs** across every Loblaw store in Ontario with SQL and Python to find which banners and categories carried the most shrink risk, so reduction effort could go where it would pay.
- **Retired the Access apps.** I rebuilt **11+** legacy MS Access applications as Python/SQL scripts. Reports ran **93%** faster.
- **Automated the handoffs.** I built scheduled Python reports (pandas, OpenPyXL, Oracle, GCP) that an offshore team relied on, saving them **115+ hours** a year.

## What mattered

This was my first time working on data that covered a whole province. Writing queries efficient enough to iterate on was half the job. The automation work showed me how much of an analyst's week goes to rebuilding the same report.
