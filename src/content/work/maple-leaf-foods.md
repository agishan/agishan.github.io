## The job

My first co-op, with Maple Leaf Foods' Plant Maintenance & Procurement team, during the rollout of SAP's Multi Resource Scheduler (MRS) across production plants. MRS is the system that schedules maintenance work against plant equipment.

## What I did

- **Configuration and testing.** I led configuration changes to MRS and ran systems integration and user acceptance testing with plant staff, turning their feedback into functionality and UI fixes.
- **Adoption tracking.** I built Power BI dashboards on SQL and SAP Business Warehouse data, tracking whether plants were using the new system and where things were inefficient.
- **Data conversion.** I automated the data conversion every new plant needed with VBA and SAP, cutting conversion time by **85%**.
