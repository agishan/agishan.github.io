## The job

My first co-op, with Maple Leaf Foods' Plant Maintenance & Procurement team, during the rollout of SAP's Multi Resource Scheduler (MRS) across production plants. MRS is the system that schedules maintenance work against plant equipment.

## What I did

- **Configuration and testing with the people using it.** I led configuration changes to MRS and ran systems integration and user acceptance testing with plant staff, turning their feedback into functionality and UI fixes.
- **Measured adoption.** I built Power BI dashboards on SQL and SAP Business Warehouse data, tracking whether plants were actually using the new system and where things were inefficient.
- **Sped up each rollout.** I automated the data conversion every new plant needed with VBA and SAP, cutting conversion time by **85%**.

## What mattered

Enterprise software is mostly change management. The system working wasn't the hard part; getting a maintenance planner to trust it over their spreadsheet was. It's the only ERP job I've had, and it's why I build dashboards that measure adoption, not just output.
